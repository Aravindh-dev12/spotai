# Phase 1 — FORM MVP

## Goal

Prove one behavioral hypothesis: **users care enough about what their Form becomes next to live, return, share and begin another season.**

## Included

1. Adult-only account foundation.
2. Life Mode onboarding: wants more / wants less / desired feeling.
3. Unknown Form and awakening progress.
4. User-submitted Life Signals with explicit evidence level and visibility.
5. AI-assisted signal classification behind `AiGateway`.
6. Deterministic trait updates in `@form/domain`.
7. Six initial shared archetypes: VECTOR, ECHO, FORGE, ORBIT, PULSE, HAVEN.
8. One Form reveal job.
9. Crew membership foundation.
10. Season and memory persistence.

## Explicitly excluded

Public feed, DMs, Food product, Health coach, background location, contact-book ingestion, live AR multiplayer, marketplace, creator economy and licensed Worlds.

## Product invariants

- The user chooses which moments count.
- AI can classify or render; it cannot silently decide identity state.
- Every Form change must be explainable from stored evidence.
- Inactivity never degrades or shames the user.
- A user may delete a Life Signal and request recomputation.
- Multi-person creative media requires explicit consent scope.

## Validation gates

Measure awakening completion, reveal share rate, invite conversion, Crew formation and Month-2 season start. If users admire a reveal but do not return to influence the next Form state, the product is a novelty and the team should not expand into Worlds.
