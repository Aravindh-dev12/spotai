# Phase 1 — SpotAI / Form MVP

## Goal

Prove one behavioral hypothesis:

> **Users care enough about what their Form becomes next to live, return, share, involve friends and begin another Season.**

The 3D/World work exists to make that identity emotionally tangible. It does not replace the identity loop.

## Core loop

`WANT → LIVE → AWAKEN → SHARE → CREW → EVOLVE → NEXT SEASON`

## Included foundation

1. 18+ account/session foundation.
2. Life Mode onboarding.
3. Unknown Form and awakening progress.
4. User-chosen Life Signals with evidence level and visibility.
5. AI-assisted evidence classification behind `AiGateway`.
6. Deterministic, versioned trait updates.
7. Shared Form archetypes; current runtime implementation begins with `VECTOR I`.
8. Explainable Form history and evidence removal/recomputation.
9. Explicitly selected media and participant consent.
10. Async Form reveal jobs.
11. Crew create/invite/join with 2–5 members.
12. Season recap and durable domain events.
13. Blender-authored persistent Form manifestation contract.
14. `@form/world-runtime` for reviewed pose/gesture → animation/ability → World state behavior.
15. First original micro-World: `SIGNAL_ZERO`.
16. Procedural rocky `O` engineering asset as a placeholder for the future intelligence/guide character.

## First realtime target

Build one impressive, bounded experience before broadening scope:

```text
LIVE CAMERA
  → pose landmarks
  → VECTOR I manifestation
  → open palm
  → VECTOR FIELD ability
  → O reacts through alien mathematical language
  → hands together
  → SIGNAL ZERO completes
  → 7–10 second Memory/reveal
  → share
```

`O` is not the user's Form. `O` is a separate intelligence/guide/world character. Form identity remains the representation of the user.

## Blender quality gate

Do not create six expensive Forms yet. First make `VECTOR I` meet this bar:

- stable persistent silhouette and materials
- mobile-friendly GLB
- standardized `FORM_RIG`
- idle/movement/duck/jump actions
- open-palm ability animation
- hands-together charge animation
- VFX anchors
- responsive camera/world runtime on a mid-range phone
- identity continuity across reveal, World and Memory output

## AI role

AI may classify chosen evidence, interpret onboarding language, generate safe narration, assist creative rendering, help plan scenes and adapt bounded World content.

AI must not be final authority for Trait mutations, Form archetype/level, consent, Crew membership, payments, authorization or World completion rules.

## Explicitly deferred

Public feed, open DMs, Food, Health coaching, background location, automatic contacts, broad AR multiplayer, marketplace, creator economy and licensed IP Worlds remain outside the first validation loop.

The only World work allowed before retention validation is a small original experience such as `SIGNAL_ZERO` used to prove that persistent Form identity becomes more compelling when it can move and react.

## Validation gates

Measure:

- onboarding → first Life Signal
- awakening completion
- first reveal completion/share
- recipient → signup conversion
- Crew formation
- World start → completion
- Memory share
- D1/D7/D30 retention
- Season completion
- Month-2 / next-Season start

The critical qualitative question remains:

> **Do you want to see what your Form becomes next?**

If users only say “cool character” or “cool AI effect,” the product has not yet validated the Life Identity Network thesis.
